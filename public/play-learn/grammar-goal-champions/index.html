Coach’s Grammar Note

How to Play

  1. Read the grammar question before the timer ends.
  2. Choose, type, arrange, or drag the correct answer.
  3. Press Shoot! to take the penalty.
  4. A correct answer scores a goal. A wrong answer is saved by the goalkeeper or goes wide.
  5. Build streaks, earn coins and stars, and improve your mastery.

Tip: Word-order and drag-and-drop questions get extra time because they need more actions.
