# Curriculum Coverage Map — Pixar Visual Edition V3

The course follows the uploaded **Connect Plus 2 – Primary 2 – First Term** curriculum. The learning interface does **not** display full scanned textbook pages. In V3, unit covers and lesson hero artwork are separated from the book scans, while the visible curriculum content is rebuilt as interactive explanations and exercises.
