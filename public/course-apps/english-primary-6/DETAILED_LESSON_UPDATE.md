# Detailed Lesson Content Update

This release expands the teaching content across the complete **English Primary 6 – Term 1** application.

## Coverage

- 6 units
- 30 lessons
- 183 vocabulary entries with meanings and contextual examples
- 120 grammar form/example lines
- 30 detailed writing workshops
- 30 model writing structures
- 60 long lesson-explanation paragraphs
- Point-by-point reading/listening guides for every lesson
- Pronunciation and speaking support for every lesson

## Lesson structure

1. What You Will Learn
2. Vocabulary Workshop
3. Detailed Lesson Explanation
4. Grammar & Language Workshop
5. Reading / Listening Guide
6. Pronunciation & Speaking
7. Remember These Key Points
8. Practice
9. Detailed Writing Workshop

The existing points, progress saving, question banks, corrections, feedback, and unit images remain included.
